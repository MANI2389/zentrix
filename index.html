ABOUT ZENTRIX 2K26

ZENTRIX 2K26 is a premier one-day technical and non-technical event organized by the departments of CSE, IT and AI & DS at The Kavery Engineering College (Autonomous).

Designed exclusively for internal college students, it serves as a launchpad for young engineers to showcase innovative projects, test algorithmic speed, demonstrate prompt engineering skills, and compete in engaging non-technical arenas.
